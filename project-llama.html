Math-Specialized Llama 2

Llama 2 fine-tuned on GSM8K for mathematical reasoning — 70% less GPU memory, 0.83 training loss, shipped publicly.

About

Llama 2 fine-tuned on GSM8K for mathematical reasoning, covering the full lifecycle from data preparation and training through evaluation and public deployment.

The constraint was memory. Parameter-efficient fine-tuning and 8-bit quantization brought a model that would not otherwise fit into something trainable — and then shippable.

Key features

  • QLoRA, PEFT, and 8-bit quantization cut the GPU memory footprint by 70% while reaching a 0.83 training loss.
  • A Dockerized inference pipeline deployed to Hugging Face Spaces, publicly runnable rather than a notebook artifact.
  • End-to-end coverage — data preparation, training, evaluation, and deployment — as one reproducible pipeline.
